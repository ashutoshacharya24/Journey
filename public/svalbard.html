Svalbard: Into the Arctic Realm

A realm where the midnight sun illuminates stark glaciers, and apex predators roam an unforgiving, pristine wilderness at the northernmost inhabited boundary of planet Earth.

Temperature -18° C
Expedition Length 14 Days
Base Camp Longyearbyen / Spitsbergen
Status Completed

Dispatch 01: The Midnight Sun

Located midway between Norway and the North Pole, Svalbard is an archipelago of profound extremes. For months during the summer, the sun never dips below the horizon. It casts an eternal gold twilight across the ice fjords, illuminating ancient moraines and isolated trapper cabins.

Massive ice wall of Nordenskiöld Glacier. Photo by Ashutosh.

Tracking the Kings of the Ice

Navigating sea ice by Zodiac under the supervision of Arctic guides. Here, polar bears outnumber humans. The silence is profound—broken only by the thunderous cracking of calving glaciers into frozen seawater.
